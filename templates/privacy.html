Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, update your profile, apply for jobs, or communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, academic details, and resume.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Facilitate the recruitment process between students and companies.
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support messages.
  • Respond to your comments and questions.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy. We share student information with registered companies for recruitment purposes when you apply for a placement drive.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@aarohansetu.in.
